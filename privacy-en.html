Privacy Policy

Novasurf ("the app") does not collect personal information.

What is stored on your device

  • Your best distance and today's best run (for the ghost and route map)
  • Sound and haptics settings

These stay on your device and are never sent to anyone, including the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

The daily leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in, your distance (score) is sent to Game Center and shown on the leaderboard with your display name, under Apple's privacy policy. The app never displays, stores or sends player IDs. Signing in is optional; you can play without it.

Sharing the route map

When you tap "Share route map", the route map image is handed to the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app never sends it on its own.

What we don't use

No ads, analytics, tracking, sign-up, external servers or in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect children's information.
